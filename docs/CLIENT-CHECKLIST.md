# Client checklist — what we need from a new bakery

This is a **client-facing** document. It is written for a bakery owner, not for a
developer. Use it during the pitch and to collect everything needed to build and
launch their site. It is safe to copy the questions or the ready-to-send WhatsApp
message at the bottom straight to the owner.

Do not add developer jargon, internal architecture, or credentials to this file.

## What the bakery gets

- A fast, mobile-friendly website that shows every item they sell.
- Customers browse, pick quantities, choose pickup or delivery, and send the whole
  order to the bakery **on WhatsApp** — no apps, no payment gateway, no commissions.
- The site works in **English, Sinhala and Tamil**.
- The owner can update **prices and sold-out items** themselves, for free, from a
  simple Google Sheet.
- Website hosting costs the bakery **nothing per month**.
- When live, the site can appear on Google and be shared on Facebook and WhatsApp
  with a proper preview.

## 1. Business basics

- [ ] Bakery / brand name (exactly as it should appear).
- [ ] A short tagline or one-line description.
- [ ] A short "our story" paragraph (a few sentences; we can polish it).
- [ ] Logo file, if they have one (PNG or SVG on a plain or transparent background).
- [ ] Brand colours — or tell us to choose for them.
- [ ] Any font or style they must match (optional).
- [ ] Photos for the top of the home page and a gallery section.

## 2. Contact details

- [ ] **WhatsApp number that should receive orders** (with country code).
- [ ] Phone number shown on the site.
- [ ] Email (optional — for display only; orders go to WhatsApp).
- [ ] Facebook / Instagram / TikTok links (optional).
- [ ] Who replies to the orders, and roughly when they are available.

## 3. Location and opening hours

- [ ] Full address as it should be written.
- [ ] Google Maps location (a shared pin/link is perfect).
- [ ] Opening hours for each day, including closed days.
- [ ] Special or holiday hours, if any (optional).
- [ ] Parking / landmark directions, if useful (optional).

## 4. How they take orders

- [ ] Pickup, delivery, or both?
- [ ] Delivery areas they cover (we display this but do not calculate fees — the
      owner confirms in chat).
- [ ] Minimum order amount, if any (optional).
- [ ] Advance notice needed, e.g. "cakes need 1 day" (optional).
- [ ] Any items that need a phone call instead of WhatsApp (optional).

## 5. Menu

For every item, we need:

- [ ] Category (e.g. Bread, Cakes, Short Eats, Drinks).
- [ ] Item name.
- [ ] Price in LKR.
- [ ] One short sentence describing it (optional but recommended).
- [ ] A photo (optional to start — we can launch with placeholders and add photos
      later).
- [ ] Sinhala and/or Tamil name, if they have it (optional; we can arrange
      translation).
- [ ] Which items are their best-sellers or should be featured.

A simple table is enough — the owner can fill it in a note or a spreadsheet:

| Category | Item      | Price (LKR) | Description                      | Photo? |
| -------- | --------- | ----------- | -------------------------------- | ------ |
| Cakes    | Love Cake | 1800        | Rich spiced Sri Lankan love cake | yes    |
| Bread    | Milk Rice | 120         | Fresh, made each morning         | no     |

We will handle item IDs, image sizing and formatting — the owner just supplies the
above.

## 6. Photos

- [ ] A wide photo for the top of the home page.
- [ ] Product photos (as many as possible — natural light, no screenshots or
      watermarks).
- [ ] Shop front / inside the shop.
- [ ] The baker or team (optional).
- [ ] Any logos or signage.

Tips to share with the owner:

- Send the **original** photos, not forwards/screenshots. WhatsApp compresses
  images — for important photos, send as a **document** or share a Google
  Drive / Google Photos link.
- Natural daylight looks best. A clean background beats a busy one.
- It is fine to start with a few photos and add more later.

## 7. Price and availability updates (Google Sheet)

- [ ] A Gmail address the bakery controls (ideally a dedicated bakery account, not
      a personal one) so we can set up their price sheet.
- [ ] The person who will update prices and mark items sold out.
- [ ] We set up the Sheet, show them how to use it, and keep a backup of the
      current prices on our side.

## 8. Domain and languages

- [ ] Preferred web address. A free option is `<bakery-name>.zuvo.lk`. The owner
      can also use their own domain if they have one (or want to buy one).
- [ ] Languages needed: English is always included. Add Sinhala and/or Tamil?
- [ ] Which language should be the default?

## 9. Sign-off

- [ ] The person who approves the final site.
- [ ] Any existing brand guidelines or marketing material to match.
- [ ] A deadline or event the site should be ready for (optional).

## What we do NOT need

- No payment gateway or online payments.
- No bank details or payment credentials.
- No hosting or server accounts — hosting is included and free.
- No technical skills from the owner.
- No professional writing or professional photos to get started.

## What happens next

1. You send us the items above (in any order).
2. We build the site and show you a preview link.
3. You review and tell us what to change.
4. We connect the bakery's WhatsApp number and Google Sheet.
5. The site goes live on the chosen web address.
6. We show the owner how to update prices and mark items sold out.

## Ready-to-send WhatsApp message

Copy and send this to the bakery owner:

```text
Hi! To build your bakery website, please send me the following when you can.

1. Bakery name, a short description, and your logo (if you have one).
2. The WhatsApp number where you want to receive orders, plus a phone number.
3. Your full address, a Google Maps location, and your opening hours (with closed days).
4. Do you offer pickup, delivery, or both? Which areas do you deliver to?
5. Your menu: item name, price in LKR, a short description, and a photo if you have one. Please group them by category (bread, cakes, short eats, drinks, etc.).
6. A few good photos for the website (send originals, or a Google Drive link).
7. A Gmail address you control so I can set up a simple price sheet you can update yourself.
8. Which languages you want: English, Sinhala, Tamil. English is included by default.
9. Do you want a free web address like yourbakery.zuvo.lk, or your own domain?

You do not need online payments, hosting, or any technical setup. I will handle that.
Send whatever you have first, and we can fill in the rest later.
```
